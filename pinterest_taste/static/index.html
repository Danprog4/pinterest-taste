What makes the cut?

Pinterest Taste helps TikTok carousel creators find alternative photos for their slides. Start with the look you want, then compare the model's highest ranked picks with the other photos found.

The bundled model learned from one person's choices: 337 photo comparisons, 260 liked examples, and 672 rejected examples.

It leans toward candid phone photos of men's fitness, food, and everyday life. Studio stock, renders, infographics, and pronounced background blur tend to score lower.

It is a personal filter, not a guarantee that an image is a real photo or right for every carousel. The score is not a probability that you will like it.

What stays local?

Photo descriptions and scoring run on your computer. Your reference photo stays local; only your chosen search text goes to Pinterest. Model files download from Hugging Face on first use.

This app is not affiliated with Pinterest. Training controls are planned for a later version.
